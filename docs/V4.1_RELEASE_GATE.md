## Final decision

**LAUNCH BLOCKED.** A critical production claim cannot be made while the
Cloudflare tunnel route, signed artifacts/checksum manifest, packaged desktop
SSH session, and atomic rollback evidence are unverified. The public Pages
deployment is a separate HTTPS surface; it does not prove the requested tunnel
route to `127.0.0.1:8080`.
